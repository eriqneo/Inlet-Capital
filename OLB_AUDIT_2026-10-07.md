# Outstanding Loan Balance Audit

## Scope and Evidence

Read-only production comparison on 7 October 2026, 22:02:19-22:04:00 EAT.
122 loans, 577 repayments, 919 schedules and three savings balance-offs were
read from PocketHost. Ten logical requests, including authentication and paged
reads. No business records or schema were changed. No commit or push.

The comparisons reconstruct the current workspace formulas against one loaded
dataset. They do not inspect the deployed interface or browser cache. Reads are
sequential, not an atomic database transaction; no loaded record was updated
during the audit interval. Current member/group ownership and lifecycle apply.
The shared calculator is a comparison baseline, not independent accounting
certification of every source record.

## Confirmed Gaps

1. **Group Profile excludes unpaid fines.** Its local calculation subtracts
   contractual repayments and balance-offs but does not include outstanding
   fines. Actual grouped accounts omit KES 4,500.00 of fines in this snapshot.
   Source: `src/features/groups/GroupProfile.js`, `calculateLoanBalance`.
2. **Group Performance ignores balance-offs and unpaid fines.** It subtracts
   only contractual repayments. Actual grouped accounts have KES 16,800.00 of
   omitted settlements and KES 4,500.00 of omitted fines. Source:
   `src/features/reports/ReportsDashboard.js`, `updateGroups`.
3. **Dates have different meanings.** Analytics uses repayments/settlements
   through its snapshot cutoff. Individual Performance's OLB remains current;
   its dates filter savings. Repayments dates filter scheduled due dates, but
   its OLB KPI still sums all scoped loans, regardless of the filtered rows.
   These are not interchangeable period figures. Source: `renderData`,
   `updateIndividuals`, `updateRepayments`.
4. **Incomplete reads can appear as financial totals.** Dashboard substitutes
   empty arrays on failed repayment/schedule/settlement reads. Reports retains
   previous or empty child datasets after partial failures; savings readiness
   does not certify OLB completeness. LoanDetails records load errors but still
   calculates its headline balance. A failed read must not be interpreted as no
   payments or no fines.
5. **Freshness is not one financial snapshot.** Financial mutations invalidate
   caches, but each view fetches its own combination of child records. Analytics
   listens to loans, schedules and repayments; Group Profile listens directly
   to members/loans, not repayment/settlement events. Reports and Dashboard rely
   on cache notifications and differing refresh paths. External writes need a
   shared event path; multiple source reads need explicit completeness/revision.
6. **Shared helper does not enforce all eligibility itself.** It accepts all
   supplied repayments, does not deduplicate IDs, and does not date-filter them
   merely because a historical `referenceDate` is provided. Callers can disagree.
   Liability fallbacks also differ: the shared helper uses the greater of stored
   liability and principal + interest; profiles prefer a positive stored value.
   No current liability-fallback difference appeared in the affected live rows.

## Other Checks and Limits

The current officer partition reconciles exactly: 61,020.00 + 1,409,370.00 +
289,400.00 = 1,759,790.00. No positive shared OLB on completed/closed loans,
future disbursements/repayments, reversed repayments or missing loan owners was
found. Excluded member-lifecycle OLB was zero.

Ten schedules contain recorded paid amounts without matching loaded repayments
or settlements. This flags missing receipt support, not proven fraud or a proven
calculation error; archived/orphaned or migrated records need investigation.
Disabling recorded schedule-paid fallback did not change current per-loan OLB.
The audit script now includes loan/status context for these checks on future runs.

The prior-month-end Analytics formula gives KES 1,567,915.00 at 30 September
2026, 23:59:59.999 EAT. This is not certified historical reconstruction: current
renewal links, contract terms, waivers and lifecycle metadata cannot establish
their earlier values without event history. In particular, an old loan's present
`renewed_to` link suppresses it even before renewal; that needs an effective-date
rule before historical OLB is reliable.

## Permanent Solution: Next Implementation

1. Define a versioned OLB contract: unpaid principal + contractual interest +
   unpaid fines; receipts split fines from contractual payments, posted savings
   balance-offs reduce the contract once, reversals are excluded, and written-off
   or renewed source loans have zero current operational balance. A new renewal
   has its own loan ID, liability and receipt history.
2. Extend one pure shared calculator to enforce exact monetary arithmetic,
   record-ID eligibility, common liability validation, Nairobi date cutoffs and
   renewal/write-off effective dates. Return contractual balance and fines
   separately as well as total OLB and exceptions.
3. Load one scoped, complete loan financial snapshot containing loans, receipts,
   settlements, schedules, settings and owner metadata. Reject partial initial
   totals; retain a previously complete snapshot with an explicit stale status
   after a failed refresh. IndexedDB stores derived data, not alternate formulas.
4. Replace local formulas in Group Profile, Group Performance and LoanDetails;
   use the same snapshot and calculator in Dashboard, Analytics, reports and debt
   units. Date/cohort filters must be labelled, and schedule-row KPIs must not
   double-count a loan appearing on multiple installments.
5. Centralize financial invalidation/realtime, including repayments, schedules,
   settlements, settings, renewals, write-offs and lifecycle/assignment changes.
   Coalesce refreshes and retain the existing 429 cooldown protections.
6. Add cross-view browser reconciliation and mutation tests under identical
   officer/account/date scope, including failure states and historical cutoffs.

No application formulas were changed in this audit step. Added
`scripts/audit_olb_reconciliation.mjs` and seven focused shared-calculator tests
in `tests/loanPortfolio.test.mjs`. Those tests establish a baseline; they do not
claim the consumer/date/cache gaps above are fixed.
