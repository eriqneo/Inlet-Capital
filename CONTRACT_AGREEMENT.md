## 1. Purpose

This Agreement records the scope, payment terms, handover terms, and post-delivery support arrangement for the software application developed for the Client.

The Developer has built a custom business management system for the Client, together with a static landing page website that accompanies the application.

## 2. Project Scope Delivered

The application delivered includes the following modules and system areas:

1. **Dashboard Module**  
   Summary view for key business metrics, recent activity, alerts, savings, members, groups, loans, and arrears.

2. **Analytics Module**  
   Portfolio performance insights, loan officer collection targets, active loan portfolio, total disbursed loans, savings base, repayment rate, arrears, and related charts.

3. **Members Module**  
   Member registration, member profiles, member financial summaries, savings history, loans history, securities, profile edits, group assignment, and member actions.

4. **Groups / Table Banking Module**  
   Group registration, group profiles, members table, Table Banking table, savings table, group KPIs, group/member filtering, group account tracking, and performance rating.

5. **Loans Module**  
   Loan applications, member/group fuzzy search, securities/collateral capture, guarantor information, interest and fee calculation, approval queue, full approval, partial approval, approval comments, disbursement, repayment schedule, loan details, repayments, and loan status handling.

6. **Savings Module**  
   Savings deposits, withdrawals, payment method capture, references, remarks, member/group selection, recent transactions, and savings history.

7. **Expenses Module**  
   Expense recording, expense listing, votehead/category handling, and expense reporting support.

8. **Reports Module**  
   Business reports including cash flow, group performance, securities, withdrawals, arrears, savings, loan-related reports, filtered exports, Excel/PDF/print support, and branded report output.

9. **Settings Module**  
   Organization details, logo upload, rates and fees, users and roles, access controls, and administrative settings.

10. **User Roles and Access Control**  
    Role-based access for users including super admin, admin, manager, loan officer, cashier, group officer, and auditor where applicable.

11. **Performance Improvements**  
    Local-first read caching, IndexedDB support for faster page revisits, reduced loaders, optimized navigation behavior, and selected realtime/polling improvements.

12. **Static Landing Page Website**  
    The application is accompanied by a static landing page website. This website has been included as part of the project goodwill package and has not been charged separately.
